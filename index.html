天 BY THE MANDATE OF HEAVEN

The history of an empire is written in more than the names of its emperors. It lives in the marriages that unite great houses, the children who inherit their parents' legacies, the customs that govern the court, and the quiet choices that alter the course of generations.

Welcome to the Imperial Archives, the official repository of the T'sing Dynasty. Within these records lie the lineages of the royal household, the genealogies of noble families, the laws of the imperial court, and the territories that together form the realm.

Each name belongs to a family. Each family has a history. And every history is part of something greater.
